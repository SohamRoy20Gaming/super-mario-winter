❄️ Super Mario Winter - Guide

Welcome to the Winter Kingdom!

Guide Cozy Mario through 4 handcrafted snowy stages, defeat Frost Bowser, and bring warmth back to the realm!

  • Movement: Use Arrow Keys or WASD. Hold Shift or X to run fast and skid across the snow!
  • Variable Jump: Tap Space for a hop, or hold it down for a mighty high leap!
  • Gift '?' Blocks: Hit them from below to unwrap coins, mushrooms, and frost flowers.
  • Snow Goombas: Stomp them to squish their earmuffs!
  • Frost Koopas: Stomp into their ice shells, then run into them to kick and bowl over lines of enemies!
  • Candy-Cane Pipes: Stand on certain striped pipes and press Down to enter hidden ice caverns!
  • Frost Flower: Collect the ice flower to throw bouncing snowballs with Shift / X!
  • Cozy Log Cabin: Slide down the holiday flagpole to set off victory fireworks and warm up by the hearth.
